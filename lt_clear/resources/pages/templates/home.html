Liver Transplantation for Colorectal Liver Metastases
Eligibility Assessment for Referral (LT-CLEAR)

Liver transplantation (LT) for unresectable colorectal liver metastases (uCRLM) has shown good results in recent trials, and the field is rapidly expanding with new studies. We aimed to develop a practical tool to select patients who could benefit from transplantation.

The LT-CLEAR tool is based on a systematic review of all protocols for LT in uCRLM. Eligible studies included published and ongoing trials. The primary aims were to identify (a) inclusion criteria and (b) exclusion criteria for LT.

Out of 27 studies, the majority were ongoing, while 8 (29.6%) had published results.
Criteria addressed patient status, primary cancer, extent of metastatic disease, systemic therapy, and disease trend.
Radical resection of the primary tumor was required in 52% of protocols, while patients could be excluded for inadequate margins (11%), advanced T (22%) or N (19%) stage.
Biological exclusion criteria included BRAF mutation (52%), microsatellite instability (22%), and RAS mutation (7%).
Hepatic lesion size was considered in 33% of trials, while lesion number in only 4%.
Extrahepatic disease was not admissible in 67% of trials, whereas 26% accepted resectable lung lesions.
Systemic therapy was mandatory in 93% of protocols, with disease stability (67%) or partial response (11%) required in most.
