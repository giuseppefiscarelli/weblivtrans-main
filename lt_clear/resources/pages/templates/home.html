What this tool does

  1. Collects structured patient and disease information.
  2. Applies protocol-specific inclusion/exclusion criteria from published and ongoing studies.
  3. Returns adoptable protocols and a transparent report of failed criteria for excluded protocols.

Systematic review summary

Twenty-six studies were included; most were ongoing, and eight (29.6%) had published results. Eligibility criteria mainly addressed patient status, primary-tumor features, extent of metastatic disease, systemic therapy, and disease trend.

Radical surgery of the primary tumor was required in 54% of protocols, while patients could be excluded for inadequate margins (12%), advanced T stage (23%), or advanced N stage (15%).

Biological exclusion criteria included BRAF mutations in 54% of protocols, microsatellite instability in 23%, and RAS mutations in 8%. The number of hepatic lesions was never considered as a selection factor, whereas lesion dimensions were considered in 31% of trials.

Extrahepatic disease was not admissible in 65% of trials, while 27% accepted resectable lung lesions. Systemic therapy was mandatory in 92% of protocols, most commonly requiring disease stability (73%) and less frequently partial response (12%).
